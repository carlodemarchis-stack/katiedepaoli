What I do for a leadership team

  • Get decisions out to the company

    I turn CEO and COO priorities into company-wide messages: who hears them, when, in which language, and what managers need to pass them on. Each one goes out with a manager briefing, FAQs and a named person for questions.

  • Coordinate across functions

    I agree the wording with Legal, HR, Finance, Investor Relations and the country leads on the most sensitive announcements: a stock market listing, new roles and reporting lines, acquired companies joining the group.

  • Keep a regular rhythm

    A weekly leadership update that the Board also receives, a monthly company newsletter, town halls and a global intranet, so people hear things from the company before they hear them anywhere else.

  • Run a team and agencies

    I have led a team across Italy and France and managed agencies and suppliers, Brunswick included, keeping an eye on cost and quality.
